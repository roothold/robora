A note from the founder

The eye of an educator. The heart of a counsellor.

I started Robora because I realised that no wealth could mask trauma. When children act out or withdraw, it is the pain inside speaking — and that pain has a name, a story, and a way home.

Robora draws its meaning from the Latin roborare — to strengthen. After more than a decade in classrooms across nations, I trained as a counsellor to help children and families unlock the strength I always saw was there. Like the oak whose roots run deep, every person carries an unspoken resilience waiting to be stirred.

Our approach

What we believe — and how we walk with you.

Healing is not just about fixing what is broken. It is about restoring the wholeness already within. Four roots hold every session, every family, every story we touch: wholeness, strength, connection, grace.

We work slowly where speed would miss something. We listen before we interpret. We honour the way each family carries its own weight — and we build, with you, the quiet, patient, structural work of becoming whole again.

How we work together

A path that honours your pace.

Every Robora journey is shaped to the person in front of us. We blend clinical insight with classroom wisdom — and, where it is welcomed, a quiet thread of faith.

  • A free 20-minute call to listen, understand, and name what you are carrying.
  • An intake session to map history, hopes, and the shape of the road ahead.
  • A tailored plan — weekly or fortnightly — drawing from trauma-informed and creative therapies.
  • Family or school check-ins where helpful, so healing is held by more than one set of hands.
  • A gentle close, with tools you keep, for the seasons that come after.
